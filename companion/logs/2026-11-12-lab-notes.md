## Barge-in: not working yet (parked)
In the lab, talking over Dr. Juni never stopped it. The log shows no barge-in events. Answers were only picked up after the question ended.

**Why:** the volume check can't tell the child from the robot. Laptop mic levels (RMS per 0.1 s, macOS raw input, no auto gain):

| Source | Max | p90 | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| Robot alone, story question | 0.043 | 0.026 | 0.008 |
| Robot + person talking over it | 0.075 | 0.035 | 0.012 |

The person was only about 1.5x louder than the robot's peaks. The trigger needed 2x, and the browser's auto gain made the comparison worse.

**Started: a word-based check.** `POST /api/listen/peek` transcribes the last 3 s while the robot talks and compares it with what the robot is saying (`director.robot_window`). The panel isn't wired to it yet.

Simulation results: Dr. Juni's real question audio, with a synthetic child voice mixed in at 2x.

| Question | Rule | Robot-only false stops | Interruptions caught |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nickname | any new word | 0 | all |
| Story | cautious (sequence check, 2 agreeing checks) | 0/6 | 4/12 |
| Story | plus "theme the robot isn't saying near now" | 2–3/6 | 15–16/18 |

**Next options:**
1. Tune the story rule to sit between those two.
2. Use the robot's own mic array, which has hardware echo cancellation of its own speaker. This is the robust fix, but it's a bigger change.
